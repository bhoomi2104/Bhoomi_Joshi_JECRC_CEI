SECTION 1: HOME LOAN POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 OVERVIEW
A Home Loan is a secured loan provided to individuals for purchasing,
constructing, or renovating a residential property. The property itself
serves as collateral for the loan.

1.2 ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA - HOME LOAN
- Minimum Age: 21 years
- Maximum Age at loan maturity: 65 years (salaried), 70 years (self-employed)
- Minimum Monthly Income (Salaried): Rs. 25,000
- Minimum Monthly Income (Self-Employed): Rs. 40,000
- Minimum Credit Score (CIBIL): 700
- Minimum Work Experience (Salaried): 2 years (1 year with current employer)
- Minimum Business Vintage (Self-Employed): 3 years
- Indian Resident or NRI

1.3 LOAN AMOUNT
- Minimum Loan Amount: Rs. 5,00,000
- Maximum Loan Amount: Rs. 10,00,00,000 (10 Crores)
- Loan to Value (LTV) Ratio: Up to 80% of property value for loans above Rs. 75 Lakhs
- Loan to Value (LTV) Ratio: Up to 90% for loans up to Rs. 30 Lakhs

1.4 INTEREST RATES - HOME LOAN
- Floating Rate: 8.50% to 10.50% per annum
- Fixed Rate: 9.00% to 11.00% per annum
- Women borrowers get 0.05% concession on interest rate
- Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) subsidy available for eligible borrowers

1.5 LOAN TENURE
- Minimum Tenure: 5 years
- Maximum Tenure: 30 years
- Tenure cannot extend beyond retirement age (60 years for salaried)

1.6 HOME LOAN EMI CALCULATION
Formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]
Where:
- P = Principal Loan Amount
- R = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
- N = Number of Monthly Installments (Tenure in years x 12)

Example: Home Loan of Rs. 50,00,000 at 9% for 20 years
- Monthly Rate R = 9/12/100 = 0.0075
- N = 20 x 12 = 240
- EMI = Rs. 44,986 approximately

1.7 DOCUMENTS REQUIRED - HOME LOAN
Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Voter ID
Address Proof: Utility Bill, Aadhaar, Rent Agreement
Income Proof (Salaried): Last 3 months salary slips, Form 16, 6 months bank statements
Income Proof (Self-Employed): Last 2 years ITR, P&L statement, Balance Sheet
Property Documents: Sale deed, NOC from builder, Property tax receipts
Photographs: 2 passport-size photographs

1.8 FEES AND CHARGES - HOME LOAN
- Processing Fee: 0.5% to 1% of loan amount (minimum Rs. 5,000)
- Prepayment Charges: NIL for floating rate loans
- Prepayment Charges: 2% on outstanding principal for fixed rate loans
- Late Payment Penalty: 2% per month on overdue EMI
- Legal and Technical charges: Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 15,000

1.9 HOME LOAN TAX BENEFITS
- Principal repayment: Deduction up to Rs. 1,50,000 under Section 80C
- Interest paid: Deduction up to Rs. 2,00,000 under Section 24(b)
- First-time home buyers: Additional Rs. 50,000 deduction under Section 80EE
- Affordable housing: Additional Rs. 1,50,000 under Section 80EEA

SECTION 2: PERSONAL LOAN POLICY
=============================================================

2.1 OVERVIEW
A Personal Loan is an unsecured loan provided for personal expenses such as
medical emergencies, travel, wedding, education, or debt consolidation.
No collateral is required.

2.2 ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA - PERSONAL LOAN
- Minimum Age: 21 years
- Maximum Age: 60 years (salaried), 65 years (self-employed)
- Minimum Monthly Income (Salaried): Rs. 20,000
- Minimum Monthly Income (Self-Employed): Rs. 30,000
- Minimum Credit Score (CIBIL): 720
- Minimum Work Experience: 1 year (6 months with current employer)
- Employment Type: Salaried (Government/PSU/MNC/Private) or Self-Employed

2.3 LOAN AMOUNT - PERSONAL LOAN
- Minimum: Rs. 50,000
- Maximum: Rs. 40,00,000 (40 Lakhs)
- Loan amount typically 10-22 times monthly net salary

2.4 INTEREST RATES - PERSONAL LOAN
- Interest Rate Range: 10.50% to 24.00% per annum
- Rate depends on credit score, income, employer category
- Government employees: 10.50% to 14.00%
- MNC/Large Corporate employees: 11.00% to 16.00%
- Private sector employees: 12.00% to 24.00%

2.5 LOAN TENURE - PERSONAL LOAN
- Minimum: 12 months (1 year)
- Maximum: 60 months (5 years)

2.6 PERSONAL LOAN EMI EXAMPLE
Loan of Rs. 5,00,000 at 14% interest for 3 years:
- Monthly Rate R = 14/12/100 = 0.01167
- N = 36 months
- EMI = approximately Rs. 17,089 per month
- Total Interest Payable = Rs. 1,15,204
- Total Amount Payable = Rs. 6,15,204

2.7 DOCUMENTS REQUIRED - PERSONAL LOAN
- PAN Card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar Card
- Last 3 months salary slips
- Last 6 months bank statements
- Form 16 or ITR
- Employment ID card

2.8 FEES - PERSONAL LOAN
- Processing Fee: 1% to 3% of loan amount
- Prepayment after 12 EMIs: 2% to 4% of outstanding principal
- Part-payment: Minimum 3 EMI equivalent amount

2.9 PERSONAL LOAN REJECTION REASONS
- Low CIBIL score (below 720)
- High existing debt obligations
- Frequent job changes
- Multiple recent loan applications
- Existing EMI to Income ratio above 50%

SECTION 3: CAR LOAN / AUTO LOAN POLICY
=============================================================

3.1 OVERVIEW
A Car Loan or Auto Loan is a secured loan for purchasing new or used vehicles
including cars, SUVs, and commercial vehicles.

3.2 ELIGIBILITY - CAR LOAN
- Minimum Age: 21 years
- Maximum Age: 65 years
- Minimum Monthly Income: Rs. 20,000
- Minimum CIBIL Score: 680
- Employment: Salaried or Self-Employed

3.3 LOAN AMOUNT - CAR LOAN
- New Car: Up to 90% of on-road price
- Used Car: Up to 80% of valuation (cars not older than 5 years)
- Minimum: Rs. 1,00,000
- Maximum: Rs. 1,00,00,000 (1 Crore) for premium vehicles

3.4 INTEREST RATES - CAR LOAN
- New Car: 7.50% to 12.00% per annum
- Used Car: 12.00% to 18.00% per annum

3.5 TENURE - CAR LOAN
- New Car: 12 to 84 months (7 years)
- Used Car: 12 to 60 months (5 years)

3.6 CAR LOAN EMI EXAMPLE
New car worth Rs. 8,00,000, loan of Rs. 7,20,000 (90%), 8.5% for 5 years:
- EMI = approximately Rs. 14,799 per month

SECTION 4: EDUCATION LOAN POLICY
=============================================================

4.1 OVERVIEW
Education Loan helps students pursue higher education in India or abroad
by covering tuition fees, living expenses, books, and other related costs.

4.2 ELIGIBILITY - EDUCATION LOAN
- Applicant: Student (Indian National)
- Age: 16 to 35 years
- Co-applicant: Parent or Guardian (mandatory)
- Academic record: Secured admission to recognized institution
- Courses: Graduation, Post-Graduation, Professional courses (IIT, IIM, Medical, Law, etc.)

4.3 LOAN AMOUNT - EDUCATION LOAN
- Studies in India: Up to Rs. 10,00,000 without collateral
- Studies Abroad: Up to Rs. 20,00,000 without collateral
- With Collateral: Up to Rs. 1,50,00,000 (1.5 Crores)

4.4 EXPENSES COVERED
- Tuition fees
- Hostel/accommodation fees
- Books, stationery, equipment
- Travel expenses (for studies abroad)
- Study tours, project work
- Purchase of laptop/computer

4.5 INTEREST RATES - EDUCATION LOAN
- Up to Rs. 4 Lakhs: 11.00% to 13.00%
- Rs. 4 Lakhs to Rs. 7.5 Lakhs: 11.00% to 12.00%
- Above Rs. 7.5 Lakhs: 9.50% to 11.00%
- Girl students get 0.5% concession
- Simple interest during moratorium period

4.6 MORATORIUM PERIOD
- Course duration + 6 months or 1 year after getting employment
- (whichever is earlier)
- Only interest payment (or no payment) during moratorium

4.7 REPAYMENT TENURE
- Up to 5 years for loans below Rs. 7.5 Lakhs
- Up to 7 years for loans between Rs. 7.5 to 15 Lakhs
- Up to 15 years for loans above Rs. 15 Lakhs

4.8 TAX BENEFIT - EDUCATION LOAN
- Interest paid is deductible under Section 80E
- No upper limit on deduction amount
- Available for 8 consecutive years from repayment start

SECTION 5: BUSINESS LOAN POLICY
=============================================================

5.1 OVERVIEW
Business Loans are provided to MSMEs, startups, and established businesses
for working capital, expansion, equipment purchase, or other business needs.

5.2 ELIGIBILITY - BUSINESS LOAN
- Business Vintage: Minimum 2 years in operation
- Annual Turnover: Minimum Rs. 10,00,000 (10 Lakhs)
- Applicant Age: 21 to 65 years
- Business Type: Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLP, Private Limited
- CIBIL Score: Minimum 700

5.3 LOAN AMOUNT - BUSINESS LOAN
- Unsecured: Up to Rs. 50,00,000 (50 Lakhs)
- Secured: Up to Rs. 5,00,00,000 (5 Crores)

5.4 INTEREST RATES - BUSINESS LOAN
- Unsecured: 14.00% to 26.00% per annum
- Secured: 10.00% to 18.00% per annum

5.5 TENURE - BUSINESS LOAN
- Minimum: 12 months
- Maximum: 60 months (unsecured)
- Maximum: 120 months (secured)

5.6 GOVERNMENT SCHEMES FOR BUSINESS LOANS
- MUDRA Loan (Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana):
  * Shishu: Up to Rs. 50,000
  * Kishore: Rs. 50,001 to Rs. 5,00,000
  * Tarun: Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10,00,000
- CGTMSE Scheme: Credit guarantee for collateral-free loans
- Stand-Up India: Loans for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs

=============================================================

as collateral. It is the fastest loan to get with minimal documentation.

6.2 ELIGIBILITY - GOLD LOAN

- Gold purity: Minimum 18 karats
- No income proof required

6.3 LOAN AMOUNT - GOLD LOAN

- Minimum: Rs. 1,500
- Maximum: Rs. 1,00,00,000 (1 Crore)

6.4 INTEREST RATES - GOLD LOAN
- 7.00% to 17.00% per annum depending on lender and tenure

6.5 TENURE - GOLD LOAN

6.6 DOCUMENTS - GOLD LOAN
- Identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN)
- Address proof
- Gold to be pledged

SECTION 7: CREDIT SCORE / CIBIL INFORMATION
=============================================================

7.1 WHAT IS CIBIL SCORE
CIBIL (Credit Information Bureau India Limited) score is a 3-digit numeric
summary of your credit history ranging from 300 to 900.

7.2 CIBIL SCORE RANGES
- 300 to 549: Poor - Very difficult to get loans
- 550 to 649: Fair - Limited loan options, high interest rates
- 650 to 699: Good - Some loan options available
- 700 to 749: Very Good - Most loans available at standard rates
- 750 to 900: Excellent - Best rates and highest loan amounts

7.3 FACTORS AFFECTING CIBIL SCORE
- Payment history (35%): Regular on-time EMI payments
- Credit utilization (30%): Keep credit card usage below 30% of limit
- Credit history length (15%): Longer history is better
- Credit mix (10%): Mix of secured and unsecured loans
- New credit inquiries (10%): Avoid multiple applications in short time

7.4 HOW TO IMPROVE CIBIL SCORE
- Pay all EMIs and credit card bills on time
- Reduce credit card outstanding balances
- Do not close old credit accounts
- Avoid multiple loan applications simultaneously
- Check CIBIL report annually and dispute errors

SECTION 8: EMI CALCULATION GUIDE
=============================================================

8.1 EMI FORMULA
EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]
P = Principal Amount
R = Monthly Interest Rate = Annual Rate / 12 / 100
N = Total number of months

8.2 EMI EXAMPLES TABLE

Home Loan - Rs. 30 Lakhs at 9% for 20 years:
EMI = Rs. 26,992 per month
Total Interest = Rs. 34,78,080
Total Payable = Rs. 64,78,080

Personal Loan - Rs. 3 Lakhs at 14% for 2 years:
EMI = Rs. 14,423 per month
Total Interest = Rs. 46,152
Total Payable = Rs. 3,46,152

Car Loan - Rs. 6 Lakhs at 9% for 5 years:
EMI = Rs. 12,451 per month
Total Interest = Rs. 1,47,060
Total Payable = Rs. 7,47,060

Education Loan - Rs. 10 Lakhs at 10.5% for 7 years:
EMI = Rs. 16,601 per month
Total Interest = Rs. 3,94,484
Total Payable = Rs. 13,94,484

8.3 FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio)
- Banks allow maximum 50-60% of monthly income as total EMI obligations
- Example: If monthly income is Rs. 60,000, maximum EMI allowed = Rs. 30,000-36,000
- This includes all existing EMIs + new loan EMI

SECTION 9: LOAN REJECTION AND TIPS
=============================================================

9.1 COMMON LOAN REJECTION REASONS
- Low CIBIL score
- Insufficient income
- High existing debt (high FOIR)
- Unstable employment history
- Incomplete or incorrect documents
- Age beyond eligibility
- Property issues (for home loan)
- Business losses (for business loan)

9.2 TIPS TO GET LOAN APPROVED
- Maintain CIBIL score above 750
- Clear existing debts before applying
- Apply with a co-applicant (spouse, parent)
- Provide all documents accurately and completely
- Choose loan amount that fits your FOIR
- Apply to lenders that match your profile
- Avoid multiple applications simultaneously

9.3 LOAN PREPAYMENT GUIDE
- Partial prepayment: Pay extra amount to reduce principal
- Full prepayment (foreclosure): Close loan before tenure ends
- Benefits: Saves interest, improves CIBIL score
- Best time to prepay: Early in loan tenure when interest portion is highest

SECTION 10: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=============================================================

Q: What is the minimum salary required for a home loan?
A: Minimum monthly salary of Rs. 25,000 for salaried individuals.

Q: Can I get a loan with a CIBIL score of 650?
A: Yes, some lenders offer loans at 650 but with higher interest rates.
   A score above 700 is recommended for better rates.

Q: How is EMI calculated?
A: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1] where P is principal,
   R is monthly interest rate, N is number of months.

Q: What is the maximum home loan I can get on Rs. 50,000 salary?
A: Typically 60 times monthly salary = Rs. 30,00,000 approximately,
   subject to other eligibility criteria and property value.

Q: What is the processing fee for loans?
A: Home Loan: 0.5-1% of loan amount. Personal Loan: 1-3%.
   Car Loan: 0.5-1%. Business Loan: 1-2%.

Q: Can I get a personal loan without salary slips?
A: No. Salary slips or bank statements showing salary credit are mandatory.

Q: What is LTV ratio in home loan?
A: Loan to Value ratio is the percentage of property value the bank will fund.
   For a property worth Rs. 1 Crore, 80% LTV means bank gives Rs. 80 Lakhs.

Q: Is there a tax benefit on personal loan?
A: No direct tax benefit on personal loan. But if used for home renovation,
   interest may be deductible under Section 24(b).

Q: How long does loan approval take?
A: Personal Loan: 1-3 days. Car Loan: 1-3 days.
   Home Loan: 7-15 working days. Business Loan: 3-7 days.

Q: What happens if I miss an EMI?
A: Late payment penalty of 2% per month applies. CIBIL score gets negatively
   impacted after 30 days of non-payment. Bank may send legal notices after
   3 consecutive missed EMIs.

Q: Can I transfer my home loan to another bank?
A: Yes. Home loan balance transfer is allowed. Benefits include lower interest
   rates, better service, or top-up loan facility.

Q: What is a top-up loan?
A: Additional loan on existing home loan. Available after satisfactory
   repayment of 12+ EMIs. Interest rate same as home loan rate.

Q: What is the difference between fixed and floating interest rate?
A: Fixed rate remains constant throughout tenure.
   Floating rate changes with market rates (linked to RBI repo rate).
   Floating rates are currently lower but carry rate change risk.
